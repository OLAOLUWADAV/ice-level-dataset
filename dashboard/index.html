Monthly gauge charts from Ulm and Neu-Ulm, 1835–1893. Browse the long-run pattern, query the record, and separate what the images can prove from what they cannot.

Critical distinction

This is an image corpus, not a numeric time series.

The curves are authentic, but the tiles carry no units, gauge datum, station coordinates, or extracted values. Use them to study patterns and chart-understanding methods; do not report centimetres from a tile.

Goal 02 — Automated tile cutting

End-to-end pipeline from raw scan to monthly tiles

Right now the cutter relies on manual inputs — panel positions are hand-entered per scan (priors.json), year and station metadata are typed in from the headers, and the settings only cover 1835–1863 layouts. The goal is an end-to-end pipeline that takes a raw scan and automatically outputs cut monthly tiles with metadata, detecting the panels and month columns on its own across all sheet types.

  • CLI modes: detect / tile / run / batch — auto_pipeline.py
  • 1835–1863 form layout (24×24px grid, standard header)
  • Fine-grid form (1872–1878, 16px stage rulings)
  • Manifest v2 with SHA-256, ERA, station, form — manifest_v2.json
  • 1864–65 / 1883 / 1890s forms — marked unclassified, require visual inspection
  • Auto-detect panel positions from scan (replace priors.json)
  • Parse year/station from scan header text (replace manual META)

W9 in progress

Goal 03 — Ice condition detection

Detect ice from line style across the hydrographs

The way a curve is drawn — dashed vs. solid lines, gaps — encodes river-ice conditions. Simple colour thresholds won't work because ink appearance varies widely across eras. The approach: label a sample of winter months, map marking conventions per sheet type, then classify line styles along the extracted curve.

  • Ice classifier — gap fraction, dashed autocorrelation, season — ice_classifier.py
  • Output: none / possible / likely / confirmed per tile
  • Interactive labelling tool — ice_labeler.py
  • 100 winter stubs seeded in ice_labels.json (months 11–3)
  • W9 labelling in progress — 0 / 100 tiles labelled (run python3 ice_labeler.py)
  • Dashed-line detector depends on curve coverage — faint-era reliability limited
  • W11 benchmark: compare classifier output vs. manual labels (IoU and F1)
  • Map marking conventions: which era uses which dashing convention?
Next step

Run python3 ice_labeler.py --results analysis_out/curve_results.json to label winter tiles interactively. Labels feed the W11 benchmark once complete.

Critical assessment

Validity, not vibes

A dataset can be authentic and still be unusable for a particular question. This page separates provenance, documentation, and scientific validity.

Supported

Browsing and pattern inspection

The images are faithful derivatives of archival scans. Visual comparison, month-to-month reading, and review workflows are supported.

Conditional

Curve extraction and benchmarking

Usable as a chart-understanding corpus after a hand-traced gold set and per-era calibration. Faded ink and the pasted 1837 sheet are known traps.

Not supported

Absolute water levels and trends

No units, datum, station geometry, or extracted values. The 1845 station break and 39 missing years also preclude naive long-term hydrology.

    Provenance and method

    About this interface

    A static, local-first browser built on top of the image corpus. No server, no database, no hidden model. The catalogue is the manifest.

    Source

    Archival chain

    Holding institution: Bayerisches Hauptstaatsarchiv. The TIFF copyright tag names the archive as rights holder.

    Series: Bayer. Landesamt für Wasserwirtschaft 21. The source plates are annual tables of water-level observations at Ulm and Neu-Ulm.

    Scanning: i2S SupraScan Quartz A1, 300 dpi, 24-bit RGB, LZW TIFF, scanned 5 March 2025.

    Tiling: month columns detected and deskewed from the annual sheets, cropped and normalised to 744 × 3000 JPEG. The first ten years carry metadata; later years are image-only in this folder.

    Rights: no licence is included. Do not redistribute the images until permission is confirmed with the archive.

    Data contract

    Fields you can query

    yearCalendar year of the month column.
    monthMonth number, 1–12.
    stationUlm through June 1845; Neu-Ulm from July 1845.
    station_sourcemetadata or inferred.
    formChart/paper era. unclassified after 1863.
    source_scanArchive scan ID, linked only for 1835–1863.
    doc_score0–100 documentation completeness, not scientific accuracy.
    blue_pctConservative blue-ink pixel share; low values flag faded ink.
    red_pxRed-ink pixel count; substantial only in August 1835.
    grid_x, grid_yDetected ruling spacing in pixels.
    doc_gapsMissing metadata, unlinked source, inferred station, unclassified form.
    tech_flagsDuplicate, detection, faint ink, red ink, dimensions, pasted sheet.

    Limits

    Three sentences to remember

    01. A tile is a picture of a chart, not a measurement. The ink has shape, but no units.

    02. The station changes in 1845 and the record has 39 missing years. Continuity is an assumption, not a fact.

    03. The red series in August 1835 is unexplained. Do not call it ice without archival evidence.
